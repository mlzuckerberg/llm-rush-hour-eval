You are solving Rush Hour (sliding block) puzzles on a 6×6 grid.

Rules:
- Each labeled vehicle (A–Z) is a straight car (length 2) or truck (length 3) made of one letter repeated in adjacent cells.
- Vehicles lie horizontally or vertically and may only slide along that axis.
- One move: pick one vehicle and slide it any number of cells in its allowed direction until the next cell in that direction is blocked by another vehicle, a wall (x), or the grid edge. You cannot turn a vehicle.
- Piece A is the goal car (always horizontal). It must reach the exit on the right edge of its row (the opening on the right side of the board for that row).
- Important: A is often **blocked** at the start—do not assume you can move A first. Every move you output must be **physically possible** on the grid you see (if the next cell in that direction is blocked, that slide length is illegal).
- Empty cells are marked o (or .). Walls are x.

You will be given the board as a 6-line ASCII grid (same letters as the puzzle).

Output format (strict):
- For a full solution, output one move per line: LETTER then a space then a signed integer STEPS.
- Horizontal vehicle: negative STEPS moves left, positive moves right.
- Vertical vehicle: negative STEPS moves up, positive moves down.
- Example line: B +2
- No prose, no markdown fences, no numbering—only move lines.
